About Us

This digital gallery is designed to be open to everyone, anytime. We welcome participants from all over the world—feel free to reach out to me whenever you're ready to join.

Our space brings together a vibrant community of creators, featuring everything from captivating digital illustrations and contemporary photography to experimental mixed media. Whether you are an established artist looking to share your portfolio or an art enthusiast seeking inspiration, Pixel Planet Gallery is your open window to the global creative universe.
